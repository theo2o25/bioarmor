## Grant Application Strategy

### NASA Grants

#### SBIR/STTR Program

**Phase I ($225K — updated PY2026)**
- Apply to: Topic in Advanced Manufacturing or Life Support
- BAA: 80NSSC26R0003 (valid through Sept 2027)
- Timeline: 6-12 months
- Deliverable: Proof of concept
- **Portal:** Register in ProSAMS + SAM.gov before applying

**Phase II ($750K)**
- Apply to: Follow-on to Phase I
- **Phase II Sequential white papers due Sept 11, 2026** (if Phase I awardee)
- Timeline: 24 months
- Deliverable: Prototype

**Tips for NASA SBIR:**
1. Align with NASA strategic goals (Moon to Mars)
2. Emphasize commercial applications
3. Show clear technical milestones
4. Include Letters of Support from NASA centers
5. Demonstrate team expertise

#### Tipping Point Program

**Amount:** $10-50M
**Timeline:** Annual solicitation
**Requirements:**
- 50% cost share (non-Federal)
- Commercial customer commitment
- Flight demonstration

**Strategy:**
1. Partner with commercial space company
2. Secure customer commitment letter
3. Demonstrate technical readiness
4. Show commercial market potential
