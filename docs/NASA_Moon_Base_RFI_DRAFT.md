## 2. Executive Summary (½ page)

BioArmor proposes a modular EVA armor subsystem that directly addresses OIG IG-26-006 single-source risk and the 60% SPE radiation / MMOD puncture gaps identified in Jun 2026 Acta Astronautica and Mar 2026 Advanced Materials. Using four near-ready materials already at or near flight qualification — DexMat Galvorn CNT fabric (ISO 9001, Aug 2026), Oxford PV 24.5% perovskite, NASA-validated Surlyn 8940 self-healing bladder (LAR-TOPS-122, Patent 11,192,667), and HANF aramid aerogel fiber (Nat. Commun. 2026, 22 mW/m·K) — BioArmor can deliver a second certifiable, 10×-cheaper, parametrically sized armor layer compatible with AxEMU and future lunar surface suits. A compact 5–10 L TENG-powered algae module (TAPED ACS Nano 2025) provides self-powered emergency O₂ reserve (not primary — 500 L needed for full demand — per Frontiers 2021), with cryopreserved Arthrospira starter (Space Algae-2, npj Microgravity 2026, 1-yr viability at −80 °C).

## 4. Technical Approach (1 page)

**Phase 0 (now–Sep 2026, self-funded):** Coupon sourcing (DexMat, Lithoz LithaLox 350, HANF), puncture rig at 8.2 psi (AxEMU operating pressure).

**Phase I (6 months, NSF 26-510 $305K bridge, then NASA SBIR $225K):** Build 5 × 30×30 cm 3-layer panels; test per NASA EVA test profile (850+ hr pressurized history, NBL/ARGOS/thermal-vac analogs):
- Leak rate after 2 mm puncture <1%/hr for 50 cycles
- Thermal delta 5.4 °C vs. cotton baseline (Xiao et al. 2026 vest result)
- Radiation screening with PVA/BA coupon
- TENG-PBR bench: target 1.6 g O₂/L/day yield, autonomous biomechanical power, graceful fallback to PC loop

**Phase II (12 months, NASA SBIR $1.275M + DOE/STTR):** Integrated torso demonstrator, NBL neutral-buoyancy run, ARGOS lunar-gravity offload, progression to TRL 5–6. EAP DEA MCP cuff (19.5 kPa, 0.7 s) as optional Phase II augment — currently TRL 3–4, not in baseline.
