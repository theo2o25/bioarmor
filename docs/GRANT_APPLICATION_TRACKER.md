## Notes

- **SAM.gov is the highest priority registration.** It is required for all federal grants and takes 2-4 weeks to process. Register today.
- **NSF Project Pitch is mandatory** — you cannot submit a full proposal without first being invited via the pitch. The pitch window is 3-8 weeks before the proposal deadline. The NSF SBIR opens Nov 4, 2026, so the pitch is due approximately mid-September to mid-October 2026. Check the NSF SBIR website for the exact pitch submission window.
- **DOE Sept 10 deadline is the most urgent** — if you intend to apply, registration on ConnectWerx AMP and proposal drafting must begin immediately.
- **NASA Phase II white paper (Sept 11)** is only relevant if you are already a Phase I awardee. If not, this can be deprioritized.
- **NASA BAA 80NSSC26R0003 is valid through Sept 2027** — this is the least time-pressured target but requires ProSAMS registration now.
- **Co-fundable opportunity:** NASA SBIR ceramic aerogel award 80NSSC24PB369 may be co-fundable — investigate whether a partnership or supplemental agreement is possible.
