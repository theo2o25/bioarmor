## 2. Project Pitch — Required Fields (NSF format)

### a) Company & Team (1 paragraph)
BioArmor is developing a second-source, low-cost, self-healing spacesuit armor system to address the single-supplier risk flagged by NASA OIG IG-26-006 (Apr 2026): Axiom AxEMU is the sole qualified lunar/ISS suit through 2031, with no backup. Team combines [FOUNDER BIOS TO COMPLETE] — materials (CNT/aerogel), bio-regenerative life support, and EVA systems. Advisory: [add JSC/DLR/MELiSSA contacts from Moon Base RFI response].

### b) The Problem & Market Opportunity
- **Problem:** NASA rents suits (xEVAS up to $3.1B) but has 1 provider. OIG warns Artemis III suit readiness may slip to 2031; legacy EMUs are 50 years old. Gaps: MMOD puncture depressurization, SPE radiation >60% dose at 1 cm, $12M/bespoke cost, fixed HUT sizing, lunar dust abrasion. Sources: OIG IG-26-006, Feb 2026 Axiom $350M round, orbitalradar.com.
- **Market:** NASA xEVAS + commercial LEO (Axiom Station, Starlab), DoD/dual-use protective systems. Second certifiable supplier is explicitly invited by OIG on-ramp candidates (ILC Dover, Genesis, Collins descoped). TAM: single suit $12M → BioArmor target 10× cost reduction via mass-producible CNT + ceramic AM.
- **Why now:** DexMat Galvorn CNT ISO 9001 (Aug 2026, 3000× scale, -99.6% cost), Oxford PV 24.5% perovskite shipping, Surlyn 8940 self-healing bladder flight-adjacent (NASA LAR-TOPS-122), HANF aerogel fiber 22 mW/mK knit-able (Nat. Commun. Mar 2026).

### c) The Innovation (Technical)
**BioArmor Phase I scope (6 months, $305K):** Demonstrate 3-layer coupon stack on 30×30 cm panels:

1.  **Outer armor:** DLP alumina tiles (Lithoz LithaLox 350, 99.8% Al₂O₃, 400 MPa) or CNT-fabric shell (DexMat Galvorn, 8×10⁵ S/m, -80% wire weight) — parametric, mass-producible sizing (addresses 2019 all-woman EVA cancellation).
2.  **Self-healing bladder:** Surlyn 8940 + Barex + thiol-ene, 2-tier healing (melt + O₂ polymerization), seals ~2 mm punctures, 10–50 cycles, per NASA LAR-TOPS-122/224 + Patent 11,192,667. *Not yet flight-integrated.* Test: 2 mm nail puncture under 8.2 psi, 50 cycles, leak rate <1%/hr.
3.  **Thermal liner:** Knittable HANF aramid aerogel fiber (22 mW/m·K, 30–133 MPa, Xiao et al. 2026) — flexible, washable, dust-tolerant vs. legacy aerogel.
4.  **Hybrid O₂ reserve (TENG-powered PBR):** 5–10 L Chlorella/Arthrospira module (TAPED ACS Nano Apr 2025 TENG self-power) as *supplement/emergency reserve only* — not primary (needs ~500 L for full O₂). Cryopreserved starter (10% DMSO + 10% trehalose, Space Algae-2, npj Microgravity 2026). Tests: microgravity fluid dynamics desk model + contamination isolation.

**What NSF funds:** Coupons + puncture/thermal/radiation screening + TENG-PBR bench demo + manufacturability cost model. TRL 3→4. EAP joint-assist (DEA MCP cuff 19.5 kPa) scoped as Phase II stretch (TRL 3-4, high risk).

### d) Commercialization Path
- Phase I → Phase II ($1.275M) → NASA SBIR sequential/BAA spacesuit subtopic + AFWERX Open Topic dual-use.
- Customer discovery: JSC EVA Office via Moon Base RFI (due Sep 8, 2026) + Axiom/ILC Dover partnerships + Seraphim Space Accelerator (rolling).
- Revenue: suit subsystems → NASA certification → commercial LEO + DoD protective gear licensing.

### e) Intellectual Merit & Broader Impacts
- Novel integration of 4 near-ready materials (CNT + perovskite + Surlyn + HANF) never flown together; addresses depressurization failure mode and >60% SPE dose gap.
- Broader: low-cost scalable armor for terrestrial extreme environments, textile aerogel for energy-efficient apparel, algae TENG for off-grid O₂.
